The Quiet Harbour

The harbour wakes before the town does. Fishing boats slide out past the breakwater while the cafes are still dark, and the gulls follow them in long, lazy circles.

By mid-morning the swimming pool by the shore is full. People come for the hot tubs, stay for the conversation, and leave with wet hair and a plan for the rest of the day.

In the evening the light turns gold and the whole harbour goes quiet again, as if it had been waiting all day for that one hour.
